Your Single Source for the Complete NEV Ecosystem

NEV3S is the Master Franchise for leading Chinese EV brands, delivering an integrated ecosystem that covers every aspect of New Energy Vehicle infrastructure and aftermarket services across the Middle East.

From charging station deployment to specialized high-voltage workshop setups, automated battery repair systems, and certified technician training — we provide the complete infrastructure that keeps electric, hybrid and hydrogen fleets moving, retaining value, and operating safely for life.

Complete NEV Ecosystem

From charging infrastructure to end-of-life battery management — one integrated platform

A unified, end-to-end infrastructure platform that powers the entire New Energy Vehicle lifecycle — enabling seamless deployment, operation, and optimization of EV assets across the Middle East.

⚡

Charging Infrastructure

DC fast charging stations (60-480kW), AC destination chargers (7-22kW), fleet depot solutions, and smart charging management software with grid integration and load balancing.

🔧

Workshop Setup

High-voltage certified workshop design, three-electric system diagnostics, automated battery repair lines (CTP), and climate-adapted cooling infrastructure for extreme conditions.

🔋

Battery Repair

Automated CTP battery repair lines, module-level diagnostics, BMS calibration, thermal management systems, and battery recycling infrastructure for circular economy.

🎓

HV Training

HV safety certification programs, three-electric diagnostics training, battery repair technician courses, charging station installation & maintenance, localized curriculum (English & Arabic).

♻️

Aftermarket

Comprehensive spare parts supply chain, battery modules & BMS systems, DC-DC converters & OBC units, charging ports & cable assemblies, regional warehousing & logistics.

One Platform. Complete Lifecycle Coverage.

NEV3S integrates charging infrastructure, service operations, battery management, technician training, and aftermarket supply chain into a single, cohesive platform — eliminating fragmentation and maximizing operational efficiency across your entire NEV portfolio.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about NEV3S and our Chinese EV franchise model

What is NEV3S?

NEV3S is the Master Franchise for 30+ leading Chinese EV brands, delivering integrated Sales, Service & Spare Parts ecosystem across the Middle East including UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, and Bahrain.

Which Chinese EV brands does NEV3S represent?

NEV3S represents 30+ major Chinese EV brands including BYD, NIO, Geely, Changan, Chery, MG, XPeng, Li Auto, Zeekr, Haval, GAC Motor, Hongqi, OMODA, JAECOO, Jetour, TANK, iCAR, Voyah, IM Motors, Dongfeng, JAC Motors, BAIC, Leapmotor, Denza, Yangwang, Fang Cheng Bao, Deepal, SOUEAST, AITO, and Xiaomi Auto.

Where are NEV3S offices located?

NEV3S has offices in all six GCC countries: Riyadh (Headquarters), Dubai, Doha, Kuwait City, Manama, and Muscat. Our headquarters is located at The Business Gate, Airport Road, Riyadh 11683, Saudi Arabia.

What services does NEV3S provide?

NEV3S provides complete NEV ecosystem services including: charging infrastructure deployment (60-480kW DC fast charging), high-voltage workshop setup, automated battery repair lines, HV technician training programs, and comprehensive spare parts supply chain across all GCC countries.
