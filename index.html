Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about NEV3S and our multi-brand EV sales, service, and spare parts platform.

What is NEV3S?

NEV3S is a multi-brand EV sales, service, and spare parts platform for the GCC, supporting 30+ leading Chinese EV brands across UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, and Bahrain. Service is delivered through the NEV3S franchise network of Flagship 3S Centers and Partner Workshops.

Which EV brands does NEV3S support?

NEV3S provides sales, service, and spare parts support for 30+ leading EV and new-energy brands, including BYD, NIO, Geely, Changan, Chery, MG, XPeng, Li Auto, Zeekr, Haval, GAC Motor, Hongqi, OMODA, JAECOO, Jetour, TANK, iCAR, Voyah, IM Motors, Dongfeng, JAC Motors, BAIC, Leapmotor, Denza, Yangwang, Fang Cheng Bao, Deepal, SOUEAST, AITO, and Xiaomi Auto. NEV3S is an independent aftermarket and sales provider and is not the appointed distributor or dealer for these brands unless separately stated.

Where are NEV3S offices located?

NEV3S has offices in all six GCC countries: Riyadh (Headquarters), Dubai, Doha, Kuwait City, Manama, and Muscat. Our headquarters is located at The Business Gate, Airport Road, Riyadh 11683, Saudi Arabia.

What services does NEV3S provide?

NEV3S provides EV sales, service, and aftermarket capability, including charging infrastructure deployment, high-voltage workshop setup, battery repair, technician training, and regional spare parts supply across all GCC countries.
