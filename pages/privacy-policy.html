Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how NEV3S collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when you use this website or contact us.

Who we are

NEV3S is a corporate website operated by NEV3S and its representatives for communications, business enquiries, and market information in the GCC.

Key principles: we collect only the information needed for legitimate business purposes, we do not sell personal data, and we apply reasonable security measures to protect it.

Information we collect

We may collect information you provide directly, including:

  • Full name, company name, and job title
  • Business email address and phone number
  • Brand or market interests and inquiry details
  • Any additional information you include in forms or email correspondence

We may also collect limited technical information automatically, such as:

  • IP address and browser type
  • Pages viewed and time spent on the site
  • Device and operating system information
  • Referral source and basic analytics data

How we use your information

We use personal data for the following purposes:

  • Responding to enquiries and requests for information
  • Managing business development, partnership, and franchise conversations
  • Sending relevant service updates or follow-up communications
  • Improving our website, forms, and customer experience
  • Complying with legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations

Legal basis for processing

Where required by applicable data protection laws, including the GDPR, we rely on one or more of the following bases:

  • Consent where you have clearly opted in
  • Contractual necessity where information is needed to respond to a request
  • Legitimate business interests, such as managing inquiries and improving our services
  • Compliance with legal obligations

Cookies and analytics

This website may use cookies or similar technologies to maintain site functionality, remember preferences, and support essential analytics. Where optional cookies are used, we will seek your consent before enabling them. Please see our Cookie Policy for full details.

Who we share information with

We may share personal data with trusted third-party service providers that support our business operations, such as:

  • Website hosting and technical infrastructure
  • Email or CRM systems used for contact management
  • Analytics and security tools when enabled and consented to

We do not sell personal data. We only share information where necessary for the services described above and where appropriate safeguards are in place.

Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as needed to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information at the end of this policy.

International transfers

If personal data is transferred outside your country of residence, we will ensure suitable safeguards are in place in accordance with applicable data protection laws.
