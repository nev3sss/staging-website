Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can apply?
Authorised brand dealers, importers, EV specialist dealers, multi-brand dealerships, and fleet managers based in any of the six GCC countries. Private individuals selling their own vehicle should use our private seller portal (launching January 2027).
How long does review take?
We aim to review all complete applications within 3 business days. Incomplete applications or those requiring additional documents may take longer.
What documents do I need?
A valid trade licence is required. Authorised brand dealers should also have their brand authorisation document ready to upload after submission. We will request documents by email if needed.
What are the fees?
NEV3S charges a listing fee and a transaction fee on sales completed through the marketplace. Details will be provided to approved dealers in their welcome pack.
Can I list in multiple GCC countries?
Yes. Approved dealers can list vehicles for delivery to any of the six GCC markets. Inter-GCC delivery logistics are facilitated through NEV3S partners.
What is the Authorised Dealer badge?
Dealers who submit and maintain a valid brand authorisation document receive an "Authorised Dealer" badge on their listings. This badge builds buyer trust and improves listing visibility.
