Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how NEV3S uses cookies and similar technologies on this website, and how you can manage your choices.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember preferences, keep services functioning, and understand how visitors use the site.

Current approach: this website does not use advertising or profiling cookies by default. We prioritize essential functionality and only enable optional cookies where they are clearly needed and consent has been obtained.

Types of cookies we may use

Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to operate correctly. They may help with security, page loading, and basic navigation. They are generally enabled without consent because they are required for core site functionality.

Preference cookies

These cookies remember settings such as your preferred language, region, or accepted cookie choices so you do not need to reconfigure the site each time you visit.

Analytics cookies

If analytics tools are enabled in the future, they may help us understand website performance, which pages are most useful, and how visitors navigate the site. Analytics cookies are only used where appropriate consent is obtained and where the tools comply with privacy best practices.

Marketing or targeting cookies

We do not currently use marketing or advertising cookies on this site. If that changes in the future, we will provide clear notice and obtain consent before activating such cookies.

How we use cookies

We may use cookies to:

  • Maintain the website’s basic functionality
  • Remember consent choices
  • Improve site performance and usability
  • Understand aggregate website usage patterns

Your cookie choices

When you first visit the site, you will be offered a simple choice to accept or reject non-essential cookies. If you choose to reject optional cookies, we will continue to use strictly necessary cookies only.

You can also manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block, delete, or alert you when cookies are set. Please note that disabling cookies may affect some website features.

Managing cookies in common browsers

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data
  • Safari: Settings > Privacy > Manage Website Data
  • Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Cookies and site data

Consent and records

We keep a record of cookie consent decisions in line with best practices. This helps us comply with privacy laws and ensure that users are informed before optional cookies are activated.
